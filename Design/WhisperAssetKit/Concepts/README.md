# 불꽃 캐릭터 재탐색

앞서 만든 SVG 불꽃은 사용자가 그림체를 선호하지 않아 이 폴더의 시안으로 다시 탐색한다. 아래 이미지는 앱에 적용하지 않은 투명 배경 미리보기다. 생성 방식은 Codex 내장 image_gen 도구다.

## `flame-painted-concept.png`

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: app mascot concept, transparent cutout. Create exactly ONE small living flame character for a reflective diary app. It must unmistakably be a flame, not a creature in a flame costume: a continuous asymmetrical flame silhouette from rounded bottom to one gently curling top tip, one small side tongue; NO arms, NO legs, NO hands, NO feet. Broad enough at base to hold a very small black ink face with two irregular dot eyes and a simple quiet smile. Style: restrained handmade Japanese stationery illustration, opaque matte gouache / wax crayon with subtle uneven paper grain and a slightly wobbly cut edge. Shape first, texture second. Two warm colors only: muted persimmon orange outer silhouette and soft butter-yellow inner shape integrated into the flame. Keep inner shape simple, not concentric rings. Compact and clear at 32px; generous transparent space around object but character occupies most of canvas. True transparent background and no cast shadow. Avoid glossy, glowing, gradient, 3D, emoji, kawaii cheeks, spikes, extra sparks, jar, text, stickers, border, scenery.

## `flame-marker-concept.png`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone transparent mascot design preview for an iOS journaling app. Draw ONE tiny living flame, unmistakably flame-shaped, as if casually drawn by an illustrator with broad opaque marker on cream paper and then cleanly cut out. A compact squat rounded silhouette with a short soft crooked tip and one uneven side flick; do not make a tall triangular fire. The entire silhouette is muted orange with only ONE small butter-yellow patch low in the center. Place a very small, charmingly imperfect two-dot face and little relaxed mouth directly on the yellow patch. Sparse visual detail, slightly uneven marker edges, minimal texture, lots of personality in proportion. Flat 2D, no outlines, no shading, no gradients, no glow. True transparent background. No arms, no legs, no feet, no hands, no cheeks, no accessories, no extra sparks, no jar, no text, no cast shadow, no sticker border, no 3D, no polished emoji geometry. Keep the character compact and readable at 32 pixels.
