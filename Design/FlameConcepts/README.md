# 불꽃 형태 탐색

세 PNG는 이미지 생성 도구의 기본 내장 모드로 만든 **원화 시안**이다. 피그마와 앱의 최종 자산으로 확정하지 않았다. 손그림 느낌, 감정별 표정, 서로 다른 실루엣을 비교하기 위해 보관한다. 특히 작은 병 안에서의 가독성과 과한 빛·질감은 더 확인해야 한다.

## 시안

- `happy-concept.png`: 기쁨, 노랑·주황, 작고 웃는 표정.
- `calm-concept.png`: 평온, 낮고 넓은 분홍 불꽃, 편안한 표정.
- `troubled-concept.png`: 속상, 가늘고 왼쪽으로 기운 보라 불꽃, 걱정스러운 표정.

## 최종 생성 프롬프트

### 기쁨

> Edit the supplied Whisper flame character into a tiny mobile UI asset. Preserve the warm yellow and orange flame identity and the small friendly ink face. Make ONLY these changes: simplify to one compact, asymmetrical flame silhouette with two or three broad tips; remove both detached side sparks; remove blush cheeks; replace watercolor shading and fine grain with two mostly flat opaque color regions and only a faint hand drawn edge wobble. The face should be just two small dark eyes and a small curved mouth, clearly legible at 30 px. Keep generous transparent margin and genuine alpha. No jar, no glow, no cast shadow, no gradients, no 3D, no text.

### 평온

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small mascot sprite concept for Whisper diary app, emotion CALM. Draw exactly one small pink anthropomorphic flame on a genuine transparent background. Its silhouette must be LOW and WIDE like a soft irregular petal or rounded candle flame: three short uneven lobes across the top, no tall central point. Dusty rose outer shape and pale pink inner shape, mostly flat opaque colors. Tiny relaxed ink eyes and a short peaceful mouth, legible at 30 px. Slight marker-drawn outline wobble, handmade Korean stationery illustration, simple like a well-designed sticker rather than glossy AI art. No cheeks, no separate sparks, no gradient, no glow, no 3D, no jar, no text, no props.

### 속상

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small mascot sprite concept for Whisper diary app, emotion TROUBLED. Draw exactly one small lavender anthropomorphic flame on a genuine transparent background. Its silhouette must be TALL, SLENDER, and LEANING TO THE LEFT, like a bent candle flame with a single hooked upper tip, visually very different from a broad yellow fire icon. Muted purple outer shape and pale lilac inner shape, mostly flat opaque colors. Tiny worried ink eyes and a short downturned mouth, gentle rather than distressed, legible at 30 px. Slight marker-drawn outline wobble, handmade Korean stationery illustration, simple like a well-designed sticker rather than glossy AI art. No tears, no cheeks, no separate sparks, no gradient, no glow, no 3D, no jar, no text, no props.
